AI adoption isn't just about introducing new tools. It's about rethinking how work gets done, and finding the right balance between human expertise and AI capabilities.

This four-step framework makes AI workflow transformation more systematic and more effective.

## 1. Baseline: understand the existing workflow

Document how work happens today, before AI enters the picture:

- Who is involved?
- What tasks are performed?
- How much time is invested?
- What is the volume of work?
- What are the expected quality standards?

Establish the baseline first. You cannot measure improvement without it.

## 2. Opportunity: identify where AI fits

Look for where AI can **generate**, **accelerate**, **augment** or **automate**.

Be clear about what AI should handle, and where human expertise remains essential.

## 3. Control: build in human verification

AI outputs need validation. Define it up front:

- Who reviews the output?
- What exactly gets checked?
- How much review effort does that take?
- Who owns accountability for the result?

Oversight must be designed into the workflow, not added as an afterthought.

## 4. Evolution: redesign the human role

As AI absorbs repetitive execution, the human role shifts upward: critical thinking, curation, decision-making, quality assurance.

That shift is not automatic. It requires new skills, clearer responsibilities and new performance measures.

## Why this matters

AI transformation isn't simply about doing existing tasks faster, or replacing human effort. It's about redesigning workflows so that AI drives efficiency while people bring judgment, creativity, context and accountability.

```
Understand the work
  → Identify the AI opportunity
  → Build verification
  → Redefine human contribution
```
